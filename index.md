## A new kind of business is being built right now.

One founder with Claude, an agent stack, and a funnel can ship what used to take a team of twenty. At the same time, entire professional moats — law, accounting, radiology, brokerage, consulting — are being dismantled by the very same tools.

This textbook is for the graduate student who intends to be on the **building side** of that divide.

The book follows a single narrative arc: **you will leave with a pitchable business.** Every chapter maps to a block of the Business Model Canvas, every lab compounds into your capstone, and every framework is introduced the moment you need it — not before.

:::{note}
**Pedagogy:** Productive struggle · Build-in-public · BMC-driven narrative · Faith-Integrated Learning
:::

---

## Course Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. **Diagnose** how AI disrupts every block of the Business Model Canvas and apply that diagnosis to a real industry.
2. **Construct** a defensible value proposition with a clearly articulated moat.
3. **Define** an Ideal Customer Profile supported by quantitative TAM/SAM/SOM analysis.
4. **Build** a unit economics model demonstrating a viable path to profitability.
5. **Deliver** a compelling investor pitch supported by a functioning product demo.
6. **Build** a working MVP using vibe-coding workflows (Claude Code, Gemini).
7. **Design, deploy, and supervise** synthetic employees (AI agents) for real business functions.
8. **Architect** a marketing-and-sales-in-a-box stack using modern MarTech primitives.
9. **Critically evaluate** which professional services remain defensible in the post-information-asymmetry economy.
10. **Integrate** a Christian faith perspective into the ethical, strategic, and human dimensions of AI-driven business — applying biblical principles of stewardship, truth, and service to real-world innovation decisions.

## Module Learning Objectives

### Module 1 — Introduction to AI Disruption
1. Distinguish the AI era from prior technological waves and explain why LLMs represent a structural inflection, not a cyclical trend.
2. Apply the Business Model Canvas as a disruption diagnostic tool.
3. Evaluate the strategic trade-offs of early adoption versus late adoption using the winner's curse framework.
4. Configure a professional AI tool stack and navigate six core platforms fluently.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Reflect on the biblical call to stewardship (Genesis 2:15) and articulate a personal framework for using AI tools responsibly as a Christian business professional.

### Module 2 — Value Proposition
1. Articulate the difference between feature-led and pain-led value propositions.
2. Apply the three-filter test to evaluate the strength of a value proposition.
3. Use the Jobs-to-Be-Done (JTBD) framework to uncover latent customer needs.
4. Identify and map competitive moats in an AI-native business environment.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Connect Colossians 3:23 ("Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart") to the standard of excellence required when designing a value proposition that genuinely serves others.

### Module 3 — Ideal Customer Profile
1. Distinguish between ICP, persona, and market segment and apply each appropriately.
2. Build a bottom-up TAM/SAM/SOM model using real data.
3. Defend a beachhead market selection with strategic rationale.
4. Use AI research tools to enrich and validate ICP hypotheses.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Draw on Luke 15 (the parable of the lost sheep) to explore how deeply knowing and pursuing a specific customer mirrors the Christian ethic of attentive, individual care.

### Module 4 — Unit Economics
1. Calculate CAC, LTV, gross margin, and NRR from raw business data.
2. Build a cohort retention model and interpret its strategic implications.
3. Construct an 18-month path-to-profitability model.
4. Stress-test unit economics under pessimistic and optimistic scenarios.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Examine Luke 14:28 ("Sit down first and estimate the cost") as a biblical mandate for financial prudence — connecting responsible planning to Christian stewardship of resources.

### Module 5 — The Pitch
1. Complete all nine blocks of the Business Model Canvas for a real venture.
2. Construct a compelling 10-slide investor narrative.
3. Handle investor objections using a structured objection-handling matrix.
4. Deliver a live demo alongside a verbal pitch.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Reflect on Proverbs 12:17 ("An honest witness tells the truth") and the importance of radical transparency and integrity in investor communication — contrasting authentic pitching with manipulation.

### Module 6 — Vibe Coding
1. Translate a business requirement into a working software specification.
2. Use Claude Code to build a functional MVP from a natural-language prompt.
3. Apply diff-review discipline to maintain code quality in AI-assisted workflows.
4. Deploy a functional product to a live URL.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Explore the Imago Dei — that humans are made in the image of a Creator God — and reflect on what it means to build and create technology as an expression of that divine image.

### Module 7 — Synthetic Employees
1. Distinguish agent architectures: single-agent, multi-agent, MCP-based.
2. Design a synthetic employee playbook for a specific business function.
3. Evaluate the ethical and organizational implications of AI-as-colleague.
4. Deploy and supervise a working AI agent in a real business context.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Engage with the Christian concept of human dignity and apply it to the question: what responsibilities do business leaders have toward workers displaced by synthetic employees?

### Module 8 — MarTech & Vibe Marketing
1. Architect a full-funnel marketing system using GoHighLevel and AI automation.
2. Produce multi-modal marketing content using generative AI tools.
3. Build and deploy a lead-capture funnel with automated follow-up sequences.
4. Distinguish authentic, value-driven marketing from AI-generated "slop."
5. **[Faith Integration]** Apply 1 Corinthians 9:22 ("I have become all things to all people") to ethical marketing — meeting audiences where they are while maintaining truthfulness and respect for human dignity.

### Module 9 — The End of the Information Tax
1. Explain the economic theory of information asymmetry (Akerlof, Spence, Stiglitz).
2. Analyze how AI collapses asymmetry across professional service industries.
3. Identify the four pillars of defensible human value in a post-AI economy.
4. Construct a post-asymmetry business strategy for a chosen professional field.
5. **[Faith Integration]** Reflect on John 8:32 ("The truth will set you free") and how the collapse of information asymmetry, viewed through a Christian lens, can be a force for justice, equity, and flourishing — not merely disruption.
